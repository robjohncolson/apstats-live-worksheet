[00:29] — 2006 AP Exam Question (Form B)
Text on screen:
"A large company has two shifts—a day shift and a night shift. Parts produced by the two shifts must meet the same specifications. The manager of the company believes that there is a difference in the proportions of parts produced within specifications by the two shifts. To investigate this belief, random samples of parts that were produced on each of these shifts were selected."

[05:37] — Part (a) - Interpreting the Interval
Text on screen:
"We are 96% confident that the interval from -0.016 to 0.096 captures the difference (day - night) in the proportion of all parts produced within specifications for these two shifts."

[05:59] — Part (b) - The Prompt
The prompt for Part (b) is displayed:
"(b) Based only on this confidence interval, do you think that the difference in the proportions of parts produced within specifications by the two shifts is significantly different from 0 ? Justify your answer."

[06:14] — Part (b) - The Answer
The answer to Part (b) appears below the prompt:
"Because 0 is in the 96% confidence interval, 0 is a plausible value for the difference (day - night) in the proportion of all parts produced within specifications by the two shifts. There is not convincing evidence that the proportions of parts produced within specifications by the two shifts is significantly different from 0."

[07:18] — Bonus Feature - Interpretation Part 1

[07:38] — Bonus Feature - Interpretation Part 2

[07:58] — What should we take away?

[08:10] — Takeaway Bullet Points
Bullet points appear sequentially as the speaker reads them:

Define the difference in proportions you are trying to estimate.

Identify the procedure you are using.

Verify the conditions have been met with evidence.

Calculate the confidence interval.

Interpret the interval in context.
