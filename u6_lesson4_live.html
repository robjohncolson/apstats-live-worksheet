Key Vocabulary

Null Hypothesis The initial or “no difference / no change” claim; for proportions, H0: p = p0
Alternative Hypothesis The claim we hope to support with evidence; for proportions, Ha: p > p0, p < p0, or p ≠ p0
One-Sided Alternative Uses > or < and looks for evidence in only one direction
Two-Sided Alternative Uses ≠ and looks for evidence on either side of the null value
Procedure For one categorical variable, use a one-sample z test for a population proportion
Large Counts Condition For tests, assume H0 is true and verify that np0 ≥ 10 and n(1 - p0) ≥ 10
