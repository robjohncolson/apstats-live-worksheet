How the colours are decided
  • Green: the endpoint answered, reports itself healthy, and nothing is lagging.
  • Amber: it answered, but something needs a look (persist failures, degraded storage, CI still running, a mirror behind GH Pages, a deploy behind the last green CI).
  • Red: it did not answer, answered an error, or CI failed.
  • Hover a tile's light for the exact rule. "Age" is how old the service's own timestamp is; "checked" is when this page fetched it.
