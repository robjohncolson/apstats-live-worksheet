Key Vocabulary

Significance Testing Determining if evidence is convincing by evaluating whether results could plausibly occur by random chance alone
Confidence Interval An interval of values used to estimate a population parameter, accounting for sampling variability
Point Estimate A single-value estimate of a population parameter (e.g., p̂ estimates p)
Margin of Error How much a sample statistic is likely to vary from the population parameter; ME = z* × SE
Standard Error An estimate of the standard deviation of the sampling distribution: SEp̂ = √(p̂(1 − p̂) / n)
Critical Value (z*) A multiplier that sets the confidence level (e.g., z* = 1.96 for 95% confidence)
One-Sample z-Interval The specific procedure for constructing a CI for a single population proportion
