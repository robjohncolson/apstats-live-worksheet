[02:14] — Justifying a Claim (Example 1)

Slide Title: Example: Sweet Sugary Goodness

Claim Text: The manufacturer claims that the bags contain an average of 907 grams. The bakery owner is worried that the bags weigh less than 907 grams on average.

Question: Is there convincing evidence that the bags are being underfilled?

Analysis:

Interval: (900.92, 912.68)

Logic: Because 907 grams is inside the interval, it is a plausible value.

Conclusion Text: "Therefore, there is not convincing evidence that the bags of powdered sugar are being underfilled."
