[00:00] — TOPIC 7.6 Confidence Intervals for the Difference of Two Means
Title slide with AP Statistics logo and a drawing of a bridge/bell curve. The video is labeled "DAILY VIDEO 1: In this video, we will show how to identify the procedure and verify the conditions for constructing a confidence interval for a difference in means."

[00:11] — What Will We Learn?
Slide lists two objectives:

How do we identify an appropriate confidence interval procedure for the difference of two population means?

How do we verify the conditions for calculating a confidence interval for the difference of two population means?

Transition slide.

Shows a picture of two spiders (one large, one small). Text reads: "Mammal species typically have larger males than females. However, there are some species in the animal kingdom where the females are larger than the males. This most notably occurs with spiders."

[00:50] — Arachnophobia?
Additional text is added: "To determine the difference in the mean body lengths of female and male spiders, researchers collected a random sample of 14 adult female spiders and a random sample of 14 adult male spiders from the genus Argiope, a particularly large type of spider."

[01:09] — Arachnophobia?
New text appears: "The data on body lengths are shown below. Calculate and interpret a 95% confidence interval for the difference in the population mean body lengths of female and male Argiope spiders."
Below the text are two data tables:

"Female spider body lengths (in millimeters)": Contains 14 data points.

"Male spider body lengths (in millimeters)": Contains 14 data points.

[01:21] — Identifying the Procedure
Questions and answers populate the slide:

How many groups? We are comparing two groups of spiders (female and male).

What type of data were collected? Measurements of body lengths in millimeters (quantitative).

What are we asked to do? Estimate the difference in the population mean body lengths of female and male spiders with a 95% confidence interval.

[01:48] — Identifying the Procedure
At the bottom of the slide, the conclusion appears: "2-sample t-interval for the difference in population means". The terms "two groups", "quantitative", "mean", and "confidence interval" from the previous points are highlighted in yellow to show how they lead to this conclusion. The words "2-sample", "t", and "interval" are also highlighted in the final conclusion.
