The previous slide updates to define the random variable in blue text: "Let C = the number of cell phone owners with a cracked screen."

The slide lists the conditions required to confirm a binomial distribution:

*   Independent trials.
*   Fixed number of trials (n=40).
*   Fixed probability (p=0.21).
*   **Conclusion:** C is a binomial distribution.

**01:58 — Mean Formula & Calculation**
The slide displays the formula for the mean (expected value) of a binomial random variable: $\mu_x = np$.
It then shows the calculation: $40(0.21) = 8.4$.
Finally, a blue box appears at the bottom with the written interpretation of the mean (expecting 8.4 people to have cracked screens).

**02:25 — Standard Deviation of a Binomial Distribution**
The slide introduces the formula for standard deviation: $\sigma_x = \sqrt{np(1-p)}$.
It substitutes the values from the example into the formula to calculate a result of **2.58 cell phones**.
A blue box appears at the bottom interpreting this result: in a sample of 40, the number of cracked screens typically varies from the mean by about 2.58.
