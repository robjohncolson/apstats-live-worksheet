📘 BINOMIAL FORMULAS
P(X = k) = ₙCₖ · pᵏ · (1-p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ
Probability of exactly k successes
μ = np
Mean (expected # of successes)
σ = √[np(1-p)]
Standard Deviation
📕 GEOMETRIC FORMULAS
P(X = k) = (1-p)ᵏ⁻¹ · p
Probability of first success on trial k
μ = 1/p
Mean (expected # of trials until success)
σ = √[(1-p)/p²]
Standard Deviation

✏️ WORKED EXAMPLES

📘 Binomial Example

Player makes 80% of free throws. Shoots 10.

Find P(exactly 8 makes)

BINS: ✓B ✓I ✓N(10) ✓S(0.8)

P(X=8) = ₁₀C₈ · (0.8)⁸ · (0.2)²
= 45 · 0.1678 · 0.04
= 0.302

OR: binompdf(10, 0.8, 8)
P(exactly 8 makes) = 0.302 or 30.2%

📕 Geometric Example

Game has 15% win rate. Play until you win.

Find P(first win on 4th play)

No fixed n → Geometric!

P(X=4) = (1-0.15)³ · (0.15)
= (0.85)³ · (0.15)
= 0.614 · 0.15
= 0.092

OR: geometpdf(0.15, 4)
P(first win on 4th play) = 0.092 or 9.2%
📱 CALCULATOR SYNTAX

Path: 2nd → VARS (DISTR)

binompdf(n,p,k) = exactly k
binomcdf(n,p,k) = at most k (≤)
geometpdf(p,k) = on trial k
geometcdf(p,k) = within k trials (≤)

"At least k": P(X≥k) = 1 − binomcdf(n,p,k-1)

⚠️ WATCH OUT!
  • "At least" vs "at most" — know when to use complement!
  • pdf = exactly, cdf = cumulative
  • Check BINS first! No conditions = no formulas
  • 10% rule: n must be < 10% of population
  • Geometric X starts at 1, not 0!
  • Syntax order: binompdf(n, p, k) — n first!
