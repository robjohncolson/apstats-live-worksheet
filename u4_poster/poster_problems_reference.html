📊 POSTER 1: Simulation & Basic Probability (4.1-4.3)
Problem: A basketball player makes 70% of her free throws. Design a simulation to estimate the probability that she makes at least 4 out of 5 free throws.
Show on poster:
  • How to assign digits (e.g., 0-6 = make, 7-9 = miss)
  • What constitutes ONE trial (5 random digits)
  • How many trials to run (at least 30)
  • How to calculate estimated probability
  • Tree diagram for 2 free throws showing branch probabilities
Answers: Simulation results will vary (~0.53). Exact probability = 0.528 (can verify with binomial later)
🎯 POSTER 2: Conditional Probability & Independence (4.4-4.6)
Problem: A survey of 200 students found: 120 play sports, 90 play an instrument, 50 do both.
Find:
  • (a) P(Instrument | Sports)
  • (b) P(Sports or Instrument)
  • (c) Are Sports and Instrument independent? Prove it!
Answers:
(a) P(I|S) = 50/120 = 0.417 (41.7% of athletes play instrument)
(b) P(S or I) = 120/200 + 90/200 - 50/200 = 160/200 = 0.80
(c) P(I) = 90/200 = 0.45 ≠ P(I|S) = 0.417 → NOT independent
⚠️ POSTER 3: Random Variables & THE VARIANCE TRAP (4.7-4.9)
Problem: At a coffee shop: Coffee costs μ = $4.00, σ = $0.50. Muffin costs μ = $3.00, σ = $0.75. Assume costs are independent.
Find:
  • (a) Mean of total cost (Coffee + Muffin)
  • (b) Standard deviation of total cost — SHOW THE TRAP!
  • Show the WRONG way crossed out, then the RIGHT way
Answers:
(a) μ_total = 4 + 3 = $7.00
(b) WRONG: 0.50 + 0.75 = $1.25
    ✓ RIGHT: σ = √(0.50² + 0.75²) = √(0.25 + 0.5625) = √0.8125 ≈ $0.90
🎲 POSTER 4: Binomial & Geometric Distributions (4.10-4.12)
Binomial Problem: A player makes 80% of free throws. She shoots 10.
Find:
  • Verify BINS conditions
  • P(exactly 8 makes)
Answer: BINS: ✓B ✓I ✓N=10 ✓S=0.8
P(X=8) = ₁₀C₈(0.8)⁸(0.2)² = 45(0.1678)(0.04) ≈ 0.302
Calculator: binompdf(10, 0.8, 8) = 0.302
Geometric Problem: A game has a 15% win rate. You play until you win.
Find:
  • Why is this geometric, not binomial?
  • P(first win on exactly the 4th play)
Answer: No fixed n (play until win) → Geometric
P(X=4) = (0.85)³(0.15) = (0.614)(0.15) ≈ 0.092
Calculator: geometpdf(0.15, 4) = 0.092
