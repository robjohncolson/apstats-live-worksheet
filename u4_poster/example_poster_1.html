✏️ WORKED EXAMPLE
Problem: A basketball player makes 70% of her free throws. Design a simulation to estimate the probability that she makes at least 4 out of 5 free throws.
SIMULATION DESIGN: ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 1. Let digits 0-6 = MAKE (7 digits → 70%) Let digits 7-9 = MISS (3 digits → 30%) 2. ONE TRIAL = Generate 5 random digits Example: 3, 8, 1, 4, 2 → M, X, M, M, M → 4 makes ✓ 3. REPEAT many times (at least 30 trials) 4. Count trials with 4+ makes Estimated P = (# with 4+ makes) / (total trials) Sample Results (30 trials): Trials with 4+ makes: 16 Estimated probability = 16/30 = 0.533
✓ ANSWER: Based on this simulation, the estimated probability that the player makes at least 4 out of 5 free throws is approximately 0.53 or 53%.
(Note: Simulation gives an ESTIMATE — results will vary!)

⚠️ WATCH OUT!
  • Simulation ≠ exact answer! It gives an ESTIMATE that varies each time
  • "At least 4" means 4 OR 5, not "more than 4"
  • Random assignment: 70% needs 7 digits (0-6), not digits 1-7
  • Invalid probability model: If probabilities don't sum to 1, it's WRONG
