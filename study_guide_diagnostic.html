Diagnostic Study Guide

Pick any unit. Choose MCQ, FRQ, or Both. Get a personalized focus list.

How this works:
  • Each unit has its own card. Toggle MCQ (fast, instant grading), FRQ (deep, AI-graded), or Both.
  • MCQ: one multiple-choice probe per lesson, checked instantly.
  • FRQ: one free-response question synthesizing the whole unit, AI-graded.
  • After you've answered what you can for a unit, click Show me what to focus on and the AI will use AP framework metadata to prioritize lessons and LO codes.
  • Use Export to download a printable HTML review sheet that also works as your save file. Use Import to restore it later.
