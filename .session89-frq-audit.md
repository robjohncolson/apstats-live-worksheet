## Open questions

1. **u1-frq-tail-comparison `supportingFormulas`**: The `.json` has only `["zscore"]`; the original `.js` also had `"empirical-rule"`. The empirical rule (68-95-99.7) could be used to estimate tail percentages for z=1, but the rubric allows using a normal table or calculator. `empirical-rule` might be a useful formula hint here. Flagged but not changed (supportingFormulas is out of scope for this pass).

2. **u9-frq-scatterplot `supportingFormulas`**: The decomposition lists `[]` for supportingFormulas (intentionally tool-free for scatterplot construction). However, the rubric's P condition says if you fail to construct the plot accurately but justify linearity, you get P — which implies the construction skill involves more than just plotting. No formula fix needed, but worth flagging that this skill may benefit from a link to residual-plot cards in a future session.
