THE VARIANCE TRAP - Read Carefully!

For independent random variables X and Y:

  • σ²(X+Y) = σ²X + σ²Y   (variances ADD)
  • σ²(X-Y) = σ²X + σ²Y   (variances STILL ADD, even for subtraction!)

Standard deviations NEVER add! σ(X+Y) ≠ σX + σY
