A veterinarian keeps track of the types of animals treated by an animal clinic. The following distribution represents the percentages of animals the clinic has historically encountered.

| Percent | 61% | 22% | 8% | 6% | 3% |

Choice A is the trap of using the percentages as counts; Choice B doubles the percentages; Choice C rounds imprecisely and rounds Birds to 14 (should be 13.8); Choice D divides equally, which ignores the null proportions.
