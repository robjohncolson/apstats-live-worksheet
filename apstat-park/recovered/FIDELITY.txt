Campaign fidelity audit — 2026-10-04

No stage currently has a complete native-fidelity certification. Stage 1-1
has targeted geometry, presentation and pit-return regression coverage.
All 48 stages load and replay deterministically for 2 and 8 players, but
that does not establish that their puzzles or mechanics match the original.

fidelity-audit.json lists every campaign entry and its large-party variant,
literal actor dependencies, upstream evidence labels and remaining checks.
It is regenerated by build-pico-campaign.mjs. Dynamically created actors
and native scripts still require separate tracing. A recovered-data label
is not a claim that every behavior of that actor has been recovered.

CORRECTED SHARED CONTRACT: WARP SENSOR ORIGIN

Native FUN_7ff72bb62ec0 constructs [0,0,width,height] and registers it through
FUN_7ff72bc16bf0. FUN_7ff72bc12370 copies those bounds; FUN_7ff72bc17170
translates them by the actor position. The downward-Y stage conversion
produces x=spawn.x, y=spawn.y. bb4e260 and bc119c0 prove there is no Y
inversion. This supersedes the earlier Y-height patch.
Warp and WarpAll share this constructor, including their InitPos variants.
The browser's colored diagnostic sensors are hidden in campaign gameplay.

scripts/pico-campaign-patches.mjs applies checked source transformations
while bundling. The external recovered checkout is unchanged. Original
source hashes and native evidence addresses are recorded in provenance.json.
Construction-time correction covers recreated and dynamically spawned
sensors; the former 1-1-only repair has been removed.

Validation:
  campaign-sensors-browser-smoke.mjs: all stages and 2/8-player variants;
    sensor bounds and invisibility on load, after one tick and after 120.
  campaign-fall-browser-smoke.mjs: both 1-1 pits, right-edge falls, walking
    off a ledge, rotating multiplayer offsets and safe landing.
  campaign-browser-smoke.mjs: 48-stage loading, deterministic replay,
    two-client synchronization and a completion fixture. The fixture is
    deliberately NOT a complete playthrough or proof of solvability.

PARTIALLY CORRECTED: GOALS (see RECONSTRUCTION-IN-PROGRESS.txt)

The original preview accumulated overlap without an opened standard Goal
or fresh Up press. The work branch now corrects these requirements. Native FUN_7ff72bb531d0
requires goal+0x3f8 bit 0 and FUN_7ff72bb68510(player,3); the latter reaches
the input-edge test (including FUN_7ff72bb6ad30 for secondary controllers).
Category-8 key contact opens the goal via FUN_7ff72bb52ec0. Command 9 also
opens it through FUN_7ff72bb53190. Key contact with category 10 consumes
the key via FUN_7ff72bb657c0.

A faithful correction must cover untargeted carried keys, targeted command-9
broadcasts, generated keys, BreakoutKey, key-goal handoffs and independent
player input edges. Merely requiring all literal Keys to be collected is
not equivalent and can deadlock special stages. Standard Goal appears in
46 base campaign stages; darkness stages use different completion paths.
