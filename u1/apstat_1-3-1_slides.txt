The following is a list of slides and visual transitions in the video:

* [00:00] — Introductory slide featuring the course title "AP Statistics," "Topic 1.3," and the lesson title "Representing a Categorical Variable with Tables."
* [00:16] — Learning Objectives slide titled "What will you learn in this video?"

* [01:08] — Data structure analysis slide identifying the individuals (50 high school students) and the variable (superpower preference).
* [01:39] — Frequency Table construction showing the raw data being counted and organized into a table with columns for "Superpower" and "Frequency."
* [02:29] — Relative Frequency Table construction demonstrating how to convert raw counts into proportions and percentages.
* [03:26] — Multiple-choice question slide asking to identify the true statement about the superpower data set based on the frequency tables.
* [05:08] — Practice Problem slide featuring a new scenario regarding a survey on the perceived risks of vaping with nicotine, including a frequency table and a new multiple-choice question.
* [07:35] — Summary/Takeaway slide reviewing how to represent categorical data in tabular form and how these tables help describe data.
