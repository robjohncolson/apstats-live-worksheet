\begin{firsttakebox}{FIRST TAKE (5 min, on your sheet)}
Is pizza clearly the favorite? Name the graph that most influenced you.
\end{firsttakebox}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.15in, labelwidth=1in, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item[\dokbadge{1}~(a)] Read the Pizza and Pasta counts. How many more students chose Pizza?
  \item[\dokbadge{2}~(b)] Graph A starts at 40. Find the visible heights above 40 for Pizza and Pasta. Compare those heights with the actual counts.
  \item[\dokbadge{3}~(c)] Which graph gives a fairer picture of the lunch votes? Explain how its scale affects the comparison. Use your counts to judge both `landslide' and `basically a tie,' then write a more accurate headline.
\end{description}
