\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ MAKE THE DECISION, THEN BOUND THE CLAIM (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
Compare the $p$-value with the predetermined $\alpha$: if $p\text{-value}\leq\alpha$, reject $H_0$;
otherwise fail to reject $H_0$. State evidence for $H_a$ in context; never report the $p$-value as
$P(H_0\text{ is true})$. Statistical significance does not by itself establish practical importance or causation.
A random sample supports inference to the sampled population, while cause requires random assignment.
\end{callout}
