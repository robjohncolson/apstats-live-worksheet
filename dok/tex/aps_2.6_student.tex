\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ USING A REGRESSION MODEL (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
A linear model $\hat y=a+bx$ predicts response $y$ from explanatory variable $x$.
Its slope is the predicted change in $y$ for a one-unit increase in $x$; substitution
gives a prediction, not an exact outcome. Interpolation uses an $x$ inside the observed
range. Extrapolation uses an $x$ outside that range and becomes less reliable farther
from the data because the fitted pattern may not continue. A plausible output does not
create evidence beyond the observations used to fit the model.
\end{callout}
