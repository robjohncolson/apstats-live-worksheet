\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ CLAIMS NEED CONTEXT AND EVIDENCE (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
A number or pattern is meaningful only in \textbf{context}: who or what was measured, what variable was recorded,
when it was recorded, and its units. A statement that says \emph{tended to} allows exceptions.\\
A pattern can describe the individuals measured. To generalize to a larger group, the people measured must
represent that group. To claim \textbf{cause}, look for an assigned comparison in an experiment; an observed
pattern alone leaves other explanations possible.
\end{callout}
