Suppose a school newspaper records last night's sleep for 12 students in one class. All 12 values are valid. For this class, the school flags a sleep concern if more than $20\%$ slept fewer than 6 hours.\par\smallskip \textbf{Claim 1:} ``The full table shows a sleep concern in this class.''\par \textbf{Claim 2:} ``Leave out the two lowest sleep values and there is no sleep concern.''\par

\begin{firsttakebox}{FIRST TAKE (5 min, on your sheet)}
Which claim, if either, would you print? Name one detail behind your choice.
\end{firsttakebox}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.15in, labelwidth=1in, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item[\dokbadge{1}~(a)] Name the variable and its unit.
  \item[\dokbadge{2}~(b)] Find the percent who slept fewer than 6 hours using all 12 students. Then leave out the two lowest values and find the new percent.
  \item[\dokbadge{3}~(c)] Which claim would you print? Use both percents and the school's rule. Explain why keeping or removing the two values changes what readers learn about this class.
\end{description}
