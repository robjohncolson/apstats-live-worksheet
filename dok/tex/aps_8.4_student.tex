\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ EXPECTED COUNTS UNDER NO ASSOCIATION (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
Under no association, the expected count in a cell is
$E=\dfrac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{table total}}$.
Expected counts preserve the observed margins and describe the null table; they need not be whole numbers.
For the course's conservative chi-square check, \textbf{every expected count must be greater than 5},
not merely every observed count. A table with $r$ rows and $c$ columns has $df=(r-1)(c-1)$.
Combining categories changes the question and needs a substantive reason, not just a failed condition.
\end{callout}
