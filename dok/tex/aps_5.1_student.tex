\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ PARAMETER FIXED, STATISTIC VARIABLE (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
A \textbf{population parameter}, such as $\mu$, describes the entire population and stays fixed.
A \textbf{sample statistic}, such as $\bar{x}$, is computed from one sample.
\textbf{Sampling variability} is the natural variation in statistics from different random samples of the same size from one population.
A graph shows the recorded results; details of data collection help us investigate possible mistakes.
\end{callout}
