\begin{firsttakebox}{FIRST TAKE (5 min, on your sheet)}
Whose description do you trust more, Ana's or Ben's? One sentence. No wrong answers yet.
\end{firsttakebox}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.15in, labelwidth=1in, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item[\dokbadge{1}~(a)] Which interval has the tallest bar?
  \item[\dokbadge{2}~(b)] In two or three sentences, describe the commute times: shape, a typical value, overall spread, and one unusual feature. Use minutes.
  \item[\dokbadge{3}~(c)] Whose description is better supported? Use two graph features to defend your choice. Explain why reporting only a typical commute time would leave a reader with an incomplete picture.
\end{description}
