\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.57\linewidth}

Suppose a help desk closed 1,200 tickets in a quarter. To investigate whether their population mean resolution time was below 12 hours, an analyst took an SRS of 24 and used $\alpha=0.05$.\par\smallskip \textbf{Leah:} ``The lower-tail test gives $t=-1.5747$, $df=23$, and $p\approx0.0645$. I would state what evidence this does and does not provide.''\par \textbf{Damon:} ``Because $p>0.05$, accept $H_0$. The test proves the population mean was exactly 12 hours and the observed difference was only chance.''\par
\end{minipage}\hfill

\begin{firsttakebox}{FIRST TAKE --- one sentence before you work the parts}
Does this small study seem able to prove that the average was exactly 12 hours? Explain in one sentence.
\end{firsttakebox}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.15in, labelwidth=1in, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item[\dokbadge{1}~(a)] State $H_0$ and $H_a$ for the population mean resolution time.
  \item[\dokbadge{2}~(b)] Using $\bar x=11.1$, $s=2.8$, and $n=24$, calculate $t$. Use the supplied $p=0.0645$; you do not need to find it again.
  \item[\dokbadge{3}~(c)] In 3--4 sentences, decide whose account is justified. Compare the p-value with 0.05, state the conclusion for all 1,200 tickets, and explain why failure to reject does not prove equality or that chance was the only explanation.
\end{description}
