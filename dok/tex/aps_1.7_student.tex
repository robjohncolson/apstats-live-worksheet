\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ CENTER, SPREAD, AND OUTLIERS (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
The median is the middle ordered value (average the two middle values for an even count). For this sheet, find $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ as the medians of the lower and upper halves, excluding the overall median when $n$ is odd. $\mathrm{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1$. Outlier fences are $Q_1-1.5\mathrm{IQR}$ and $Q_3+1.5\mathrm{IQR}$. Mean and SD are nonresistant: extremes affect them more. Median and IQR are resistant. Use the distribution's shape to choose a pair.
\end{callout}
