\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ MEAN AND STANDARD DEVIATION (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
For a discrete random variable, $\mu_X=\sum xP(X=x)$ and $\sigma_X=\sqrt{\sum(x-\mu_X)^2P(X=x)}$. For $n$ equally likely outcomes, divide the sum of squared distances from the mean by $n$, then take the square root. The mean is a long-run average; the standard deviation describes a typical distance from it, in the variable's units. Add probabilities of outcomes that miss a deadline.
\end{callout}
