\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ USING A NORMAL MODEL (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
A normal curve is \textbf{mound-shaped and symmetric}. Its parameters are the population mean $\mu$ and
population standard deviation $\sigma$. Check the data's shape before using the model.\
Empirical rule: about $68\%$ is within $1\sigma$, $95\%$ within $2\sigma$, and $99.7\%$ within $3\sigma$.\
Standardize with $z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$. A normal-table answer describes the \emph{model}; it describes the data
only when the model is appropriate.
\end{callout}
