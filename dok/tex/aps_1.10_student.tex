\begin{callout}{\IconBook\ USING A NORMAL MODEL (keep on the page)}{calloutgreen}
A normal curve is mound-shaped and symmetric, with population mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$. Check whether the data's shape fits the model. About $68\%$, $95\%$, and $99.7\%$ lie within 1, 2, and 3 standard deviations. Standardize with $z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$. Table A gives area to the left; right area is 1 minus left area. Observed percent is $100\times\text{count}/\text{total}$. A model's calculated area need not match the observed data.
\end{callout}
