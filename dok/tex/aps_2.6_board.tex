  \item[\dokbadge{1}~(a)] Identify the explanatory and response variables, slope, intercept, and observed payload range.
  \item[\dokbadge{2}~(b)] Interpret the slope. Predict duration at 1.8 and 3.5 kg, classify each prediction, and calculate the modeled buffer above 12 minutes.
  \item[\dokbadge{3}~(c)] Evaluate both approaches using the observed range, the distance from 3.5 kg to that range, the modeled buffer you computed, and the in-range plot. \end{description}
