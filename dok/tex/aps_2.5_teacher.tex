\textit{Rules callout ``READ r WITH THE PLOT AND DESIGN (keep on the page)'' is printed on the student sheet.}\par\medskip

\dokbadge{1}~\textbf{(a)} State the range and units of $r$, and interpret its sign and magnitude.\par
\answer{Correlation is unit-free and lies from $-1$ to $1$. The positive value near 1 summarizes a strong positive linear association for all eight observed centers, subject to checking the plot.}

\dokbadge{2}~\textbf{(b)} Calculate $r$ for all eight centers and for the first seven as a labeled sensitivity analysis. Describe the plot's structure.\par
\answer{For all eight, $r=0.9223783\approx0.922$; for the first seven, $r=0.0949158\approx0.095$. Seven points form a compact cloud with little linear tendency, while $(20,90)$ is separated at the upper right. The second value is a sensitivity analysis, not a reason to hide that point.}

\dokbadge{3}~\textbf{(c)} Recommend Omar's or Tessa's approach. Reconcile both correlations with the plot, state what the full value supports, use the observational design to assess the forecast, and explain one decision risk from overreaching.\par
\answer{Tessa's approach is warranted. The full $r=0.922$ accurately summarizes all eight centers, but $r\approx0.095$ for the compact cluster shows that the apparent linear pattern is highly sensitive to $(20,90)$ and is not typical of the other seven. Because centers were observed rather than assigned posting levels, confounding prevents a causal forecast. Omar's approach could prompt spending on posts with an unsupported attendance guarantee.}
